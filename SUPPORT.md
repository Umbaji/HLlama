# Support

HLlama is the WhatsApp assistant of Umbaji for women's sexual and reproductive
health in Togo and Benin.

## For the people who write to the assistant

The assistant is not a doctor. It gives general information, it says when a
situation needs a professional, and it can send the emergency numbers of your
country, or look for the nearest health service. If you are in danger, call the
emergency services: they do not wait for a chat.

## For the people who run it

- The health data belongs to the people who wrote it: it is encrypted at rest,
  it is not logged, and it is not sent to a third party in a form that
  identifies them.
- The documentary base (`backend/content/knowledge/`) holds what the assistant
  may quote, with its source and its date. The backoffice scans it and shows the
  differences; a document is written only after somebody read it.
- A security issue is not written in a public issue: see `SECURITY.md`.
