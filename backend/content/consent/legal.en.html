Welcome to HLlama

HLlama is a digital assistant developed by Umbaji to make information and certain services easier to reach, in particular on subjects related to health, well-being and the needs of women and girls.

HLlama uses artificial intelligence technologies to understand your requests and provide you with answers.

Before you begin, please read the information below. It explains how HLlama works, which information may be used, and what your rights are.

Using HLlama is free and voluntary.

1 What is HLlama for?

HLlama lets you ask questions, obtain information and reach useful guidance or resources, depending on the features available.

HLlama is an information and assistance tool. It does not replace a doctor, a health professional or any other qualified professional.

Because the answers are generated with artificial intelligence technologies, they may sometimes be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuited to your particular situation.

In the event of a medical emergency, violence, abuse or immediate danger to you or to another person, please contact a professional or a competent service directly.

2 How are your conversations handled?

When you exchange messages with HLlama, the content of your message is processed automatically in order to understand your request and produce an answer.

The Umbaji team does not read your conversations with HLlama.

The content of your conversations is not used to train HLlama and is not used to train other artificial intelligence models.

Some conversations may naturally touch on personal or sensitive subjects, in particular health or private life. That information is used only as far as necessary for HLlama to work and to provide the answer you asked for.

We recommend that you do not share information that identifies you directly when it is not needed, in particular your full name, your home address, your passwords or your banking details.

3 What other information may be collected?

In order to understand how HLlama is used, to monitor it and to improve the service, some information may be collected separately from the content of your conversations.

This may include your age range, your sex, the language you use, your general geographic area, the date and time of use, how often you use HLlama, the features used, certain technical information needed for the service to work, as well as the answers you choose to give in satisfaction questionnaires or forms.

Only information useful to the stated purposes may be collected.

4 Why is this information used?

This information is used to keep HLlama working and secure, to understand how the service is used, to measure its usefulness and its performance, to identify the difficulties users run into, to improve the features offered, and to monitor and evaluate the service.

It may also be used to produce statistics and reports on the use of HLlama.

Where possible, data used for statistics or reporting is anonymised or aggregated in order to limit the identification of users.

This information is not used to reconstruct or read your conversations with HLlama.

5 Who is responsible for your data?

The controller of the data collected through HLlama is:

Umbaji
Contact: contact@umbaji.org

Where technical providers take part in running HLlama, they may process the information they can access only as far as necessary to provide their services, and in line with the applicable confidentiality and security obligations.

Umbaji does not sell your personal data.

6 How long is information kept?

The personal information collected is kept only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was gathered.

Retention period for monitoring and statistical data: 72 months from your last exchange with HLlama, or sooner if you ask for your data to be deleted.

At the end of that period, the information is deleted or, where possible, anonymised, unless longer retention is allowed or required by law.

7 Where may your information be processed?

Some technical services needed for HLlama to work may be provided by suppliers using infrastructure located in Togo or abroad.

Where personal data must be transferred or processed outside Togo, Umbaji ensures that the processing is carried out in line with the requirements of the applicable Togolese data protection legislation.

8 Protecting your information

Umbaji puts in place measures intended to protect the information processed against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, misuse or disclosure.

Access to the data is limited to the people or providers who need it to run, secure or monitor the service, and who are bound by the applicable confidentiality obligations.

9 Your rights

Under Togolese legislation on the protection of personal data, you may in particular ask whether personal data concerning you is being processed, ask to access it, ask for inaccurate or incomplete information to be corrected and, where the conditions set by law are met, ask for it to be deleted or object to certain processing.

You can exercise your rights by contacting Umbaji at: contact@umbaji.org

You may also refer the matter to the competent Togolese authority for the protection of personal data if you consider that your rights have not been respected.

10 Your freedom of choice

You are free to accept or to refuse these terms.

Refusing has no consequence other than not being able to use HLlama.

You may also stop using HLlama at any time.

For users who are minors, specific protective measures may apply where the law or the nature of the service requires it.
